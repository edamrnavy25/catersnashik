SERVICES WE OFFER

Planning

At Sunline Equipment, we begin every project with a detailed conceptual plan that transforms your kitchen vision into a precise and functional layout. Understanding that each food service operation is unique, our commercial kitchen planning is customized to match your specific workflow and operational needs.

The planning process includes detailed kitchen layouts, equipment drawings, and complete service schematics covering civil work, electrical, plumbing, and drainage. Every aspect is carefully coordinated to ensure efficiency, safety, and compliance with industry standards.

Manufacturing

At Sunline Equipment, we specialize in crafting high-quality mega kitchen equipment with precision and durability. Each product is manufactured using advanced technology and premium-grade materials to ensure long-lasting performance and safety.

Our skilled team follows strict quality control processes at every stage—from raw material selection to fabrication and finishing. We combine innovation with robust engineering to produce equipment that meets the unique demands of modern commercial kitchens.

Installation

At Sunline Equipment, we provide professional installation services to ensure your kitchen equipment operates flawlessly from day one. Our expert team carefully handles every unit, following precise layouts and safety standards for seamless integration into your kitchen.

We coordinate with your existing civil, electrical, plumbing, and drainage setups to guarantee smooth functionality. Each installation is executed with attention to detail, minimizing downtime and maximizing efficiency.

After Sales Services

At Sunline Equipment, our commitment doesn't end with delivery and installation — we provide comprehensive after-sales support to ensure your kitchen runs smoothly. Our dedicated service team is always available for maintenance, troubleshooting, and timely repairs to minimize downtime.

We offer guidance on equipment operation, preventive care, and spare parts availability to maximize the life and performance of your products. With prompt and reliable support, we help your commercial kitchen maintain peak efficiency and productivity.

Whether you're planning a new restaurant, upgrading a hotel kitchen, or setting up a food production unit, Sunline Equipments is here to help you every step of the way.
